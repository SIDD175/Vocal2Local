# Current Limitations

Vocal2Local is currently at a prototype stage, and there are certain limitations that are acknowledged at this point.

- The prototype focuses on discovery and visibility, not on payments or deliveries.
- Vendor locations are represented conceptually and are not yet optimized for high accuracy.
- Scalability, moderation, and misuse prevention are not fully implemented in this stage.
- The system assumes basic smartphone access for vendors.

These limitations are intentional at this stage, as the goal is to validate the core idea and usefulness before expanding features.
