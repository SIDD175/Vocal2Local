# Vocal2Local

Vocal2Local is a simple hyperlocal discovery platform built to solve a very common problem we see around us every day — the lack of visibility of local street vendors.

In almost every Indian locality, vendors like sabzi sellers, fruit sellers, and small roadside thela owners are essential. Yet, most of the time, customers don’t know when they are nearby, and vendors lose customers simply because they are not visible at the right moment.

Vocal2Local focuses only on solving this visibility gap.

---

## The Problem

Street vendors usually operate at flexible times and locations. Their presence depends heavily on chance encounters.

For customers — especially students, migrants, or people new to a locality — it is difficult to know:
- Which vendors operate nearby
- When they are active
- Whether it is worth stepping out at that moment

Existing digital platforms are mainly focused on delivery, commissions, and formal onboarding, which are not suitable for most small vendors.

---

## Our Idea

Vocal2Local is designed as a **map-first discovery app**.

Instead of ordering or delivery, the app simply shows which vendors are active nearby in real time, within walking distance. Vendors can mark themselves active, and users can discover them on the map instantly.

The goal is to make local discovery simple, honest, and useful — without adding unnecessary complexity.

---

## How It Works

- Users open the app and see nearby vendors on a map
- Only vendors who are active at that moment are shown
- Tapping on a vendor shows basic details like distance and usual availability
- Vendors register once and can mark themselves active or inactive using a very simple vendor mode

There are no payments, no ratings, and no commissions involved.

---

## Key Features

- Real-time vendor visibility
- Map-based local discovery
- Walking-distance focused results
- Simple vendor availability toggle
- Designed for low digital complexity

## Impact and Usefulness

Vocal2Local can:
- Help small vendors increase visibility without depending on large platforms
- Make it easier for people to discover local sellers nearby
- Strengthen everyday connections within neighbourhoods

The idea is intentionally kept simple so that it can scale gradually and remain accessible.
